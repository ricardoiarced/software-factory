# Specification traceability

Every normative statement of the final Factory specification (the resolution
of issue #4, "Assemble the implementation-ready Factory specification") maps
to at least one named test or certification assertion below. Domain terms
carry the meanings fixed in `CONTEXT.md`.

`tests/test_traceability.py` enforces this mapping in both directions: every
row names tests that exist, every acceptance criterion has a row, and no test
exists in `tests/` or `certification/` without a statement that requires it.
A behavioral or security regression adds a focused, named test together with
the statement it holds in place; coverage percentages are never a substitute.

Statement identifiers cite the specification section they restate (`S1` is
section 1, "Product contract"; `S7.3` is the third statement of section 7).
`AC-01` through `AC-12` are the specification's final acceptance criteria.
The certification column names the assertions of the real-host suite in
`certification/test_exact_tuple.py`: its single test plus the named evidence
properties and production checks of its emitted report.
